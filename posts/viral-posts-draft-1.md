Started watching Barcelona in 2019.

I missed Messi's prime. I missed Pep's tiki-taka. I missed the treble-winning era. I didn't grow up with the Ronaldinho magic or the Xavi-Iniesta midfield.

But I caught the rebuild. The Fati hype. Pedri breaking through at 17. Gavi running like he had something to prove. Lamine Yamal becoming the youngest scorer in Euros history.

People say "you had to be there" when talking about peak Barca. Maybe. But I'm building my own era of memories. And honestly? Watching these kids grow into world-beaters might be even more special.

This is MY Barcelona. And I wouldn't trade it for anything 💙❤️
