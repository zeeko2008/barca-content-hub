# Viral Post 2: The Messi Departure

**Target:** Facebook
**Best time to post:** 7-9 PM
**Hashtags:** #Barça #FCBarcelona #LaMasia #Culers

---

The Messi to PSG move broke something in me.

I started watching Barcelona in 2019. My first proper season as a fan was 2019-20. And within a year, the greatest player ever left the club I was just beginning to love.

I never got to experience "peak Messi at Camp Nou." I only watched the highlights. I saw the magic in clips, not in real time.

But you know what? It made me a different kind of fan. I stopped taking greatness for granted. I started appreciating every young player coming through La Masia because I know what it's like to lose legends.

Pedri, Gavi, Lamine Yamal, Fermin Lopez, Cubarsi — I'm watching the NEXT era being built. And I'm here for all of it.

Sometimes you don't get the golden generation. Sometimes you get the seeds being planted. And that's special too 🌱
